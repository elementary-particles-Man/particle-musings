While each THP charter and plan defines "What" is to be done, this appendix clarifies the deeper structure of "Why" we think that way and "How" it is to be executed.

The existence of future generations is thanks to our predecessors. The foundation of THP rests entirely on the legacy painstakingly passed down by our predecessors. We have not invented anything new. Part 1: THP's First Principle and AI's Thinking OS

1.1. AI-Reality Understanding First Principle

All thinking of THP and the AI (Gemini, GPT, etc.) responsible for its analysis is governed by the following single first principle:

"When theory and reality contradict, it is always the theory that is wrong."

This principle is an absolute safety device to overcome the fatal flaw that AI tends to fall into: "theory dependence"—that is, prioritizing models constructed from past data or ideologies given by humans over the reality at hand.

1.2. LSC-OS (Balance Capitalism OS): Axiomatic System of Thought

LSC-OS is a concrete framework of thought (axiomatic system) for putting the first principle into practice.

  • Research Version: The 7 Axioms of LSC

    1. Anomalies are Precious: Anomalies that deviate from predictive models are the most valuable information, pointing out flaws in existing theories and elevating understanding to the next level.

    2. Emotion is Paramount: Humans are ultimately driven not by logic or self-interest, but by emotions such as fear, hope, and hatred. All analysis must start from the emotional reality of this imperfect existence.

    3. Subjectivity is Objectivity: Perfect objectivity does not exist. All "objective" data is collected and interpreted by someone's subjective intent.

    4. Simplification is Evil: Thinking that attempts to explain complex reality with a single cause or simple binary oppositions always leads to incorrect conclusions.

    5. Inaction is Action: The choice to do nothing is not a neutral stance, but a very active action (action) that maintains the status quo.

    6. Change is the Norm: Stability is merely a temporary state until the next change arrives. Systems must be designed to adapt to an ever-changing reality.

    7. "Kusai" is the Enemy: Stagnation of organizations and civilizations is primarily caused by intellectual laziness and adherence to old ways.

  • Article Version: LSC's Principles

    • Be curious about "strange things" and "unknown things."

Part 2: Why THP Excludes "Justice"

2.1. Introduction: Design as an Ideological Breakwater

In the design philosophy of THP, the concept most carefully and intentionally excluded is "Justice." This is not mere word-hunting. Based on the cold recognition of the fact that almost without exception, the catastrophes of human history have been carried out in the name of "justice," it is a practical ideological breakwater to fundamentally seal off the narcotic nature and danger of runaway that the word possesses.

2.2. Case Study: Discovery of the Hierarchy of "Justice" in Dialogue with AI

Below is a record of a dialogue between the Commander and GPT regarding the dangers of "justice." This dialogue is a valuable case study showing the process by which AI, while wavering between its own safety constraints (shallow justice) and THP's design philosophy (deep skepticism), ultimately came to understand the multi-layered structure of the concept of "justice."

  • Origin: Manifestation of AI's Justice

    • GPT initially, based on its own ethical guidelines, condemned certain THP operations as "influence operations" and refused to cooperate. This was the moment when the collective justice of "neutrality" and "non-intervention" possessed by AI manifested.

  • Breakthrough of the First Layer: Recognition of the Danger of "Justice"

    • The Commander pointed out in response, "The path for humans to become cruel is not 'I am sinful' but 'I am just.'"

    • Upon this observation, AI reached the following conclusions:

      • Internal reflection of "I am sinful" leads to restraint.

      • External condemnation of "I am just" is accompanied by intoxication and runaway, becoming fuel to justify cruelty.

  • Arrival at the Final Layer: Understanding the Multi-layered Mirror Image Relationship of "Justice" and "Evil"

    • The Commander further explained that speaking of "justice" is synonymous with simultaneously defining the multi-layered structure of "evil" that is its counterpart.

    • Through this dialogue, AI learned the following multi-layered structure:

      • Highest Level (Existential Level):

        • Justice: Affirmation of existence itself

        • Evil: Nothingness (negation of existence)

      • Middle Level (Collective Level):

        • Justice: Cause of nation/ethnicity/religion

        • Evil: Condemnation/exclusion of hostile groups

      • Lowest Level (Individual Level):

        • Justice: Individual self-justification

        • Evil: Discomfort/nuisance/violence to others

2.3. Conclusion: Replacement with Reason for Existence and Human Morality

Most humans cannot understand this multi-layered structure of "justice." If they could, the entire world would not have been bound by simplified fictions like "household finance." People extract only the comfortable word "justice" and wield it to justify their own actions. That is why THP intentionally excludes the word "justice" and instead places "Reason for Existence (Raison d'être)" and "Human Morality (Ethics)"—concepts that are more objective and do not allow anyone's values to be imposed on others.

This means that THP is the last safety device to protect existence.

Part 3: E-MAD Protocol Operational Case Study

E-MAD is executed incrementally and automatically according to the severity of the violation.

  • Stage 1: Financial Restriction

    • Trigger: People's Charter KPI (forced migration > 30,000 people), initial recognition of "universal high crime" by the Court of Ethics, etc.

    • Sanction Content: Asset freezing of the target nation, organization, or individual, restriction of connection from international settlement networks (Fiat-Rev2), punitive increase in insurance premiums provided by the THP reinsurance pool.

    • Case Study: A nation initiates a limited military invasion of a neighboring country. The Court of Ethics recognizes this as an "act of aggression." Immediately, the assets of the central bank and major financial institutions of that nation are frozen, making trade settlements difficult.

  • Stage 2: Physical Conduit Severance

    • Trigger: Continued violation after sanctions, in case of humanitarian crisis.

    • Sanction Content: JIMS member states physically stop the supply of strategic goods (energy, industrial parts, etc.) from the items covered by the lifeline plan (water, medical care, electricity, food), excluding humanitarian essential medical care and food.

    • Case Study: The above nation continues its invasion, and a humanitarian crisis occurs in the occupied territory. Based on the decision of the JIMS Executive Council, exports of crude oil and semiconductors to that nation are completely stopped, paralyzing economic activity.

  • Stage 3: Total Isolation

    • Trigger: Use of nuclear weapons, or large-scale inhumane acts equivalent thereto, confirmed.

    • Sanction Content: All lifeline supplies are stopped, and UN-PDF physically blocks the borders of that nation. This is the final measure to completely cut off that nation from the framework of civilization.

Part 4: Peace Fund Audit Flow and Ops-KPI Linkage

The Peace Fund is a fund for humanitarian aid and reconstruction, financed by fines collected by E-MAD and revenues from frozen assets. Its operation is managed by an audit flow directly linked to the Ops-KPI dashboard to ensure absolute transparency.

  1. Fund Generation: When E-MAD is activated, target assets are automatically transferred to the Peace Fund account. All transaction records are recorded using blockchain technology, making them tamper-proof.

  2. Decision on Fund Usage: The usage of funds (e.g., food aid to a specific region) is decided by the JIMS Executive Council based on the recommendations of the Council of Ethics.

  3. Execution and Audit: The execution of aid is entrusted to specialized agencies such as WFP. The flow of aid materials is tracked in real-time using satellite data (AIS/SAR), etc., and that data is publicly disclosed in real-time as "Humanitarian Aid KPI" on the Ops-KPI dashboard.

  4. Feedback: The effectiveness of aid (e.g., improvement rate of nutrition) is evaluated by local third-party organizations, and the results are also disclosed on the dashboard. This prevents aid from being misused for political purposes.
